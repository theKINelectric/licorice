# BEING continuity development trial — 21 October 2026

The native BEING memory implementation now supports deliberate stance, structured
relationships/promises, preserved concept meanings and optional bounded reflection.
This small trial checks whether that state changes action selection after context
reset. It does not evaluate personality, consciousness or memory-benchmark rank.

## Registered comparison

Ten checkpoints cover four synthetic trajectories: retain a tested method under
a weak contrary opinion, revise it after stronger work evidence; remember an
active/fulfilled/released promise; attribute an imported climbing episode to its
author; and recover different adopted working practices for two relationships
without leaking either into a third. Every native operation ran in a fresh
process. The later verifier reconstructed all ten journals and reproduced their
snapshots and packets using the final binary.

Each checkpoint was answered in a fresh GLM-5.3-Flash request under three
conditions. No memory received no earlier history. The episodic baseline received
whole chronological attributed events, including actual stance decisions and
commitment transitions, under the same cap. BEING received the current attributed
projection with its sources. Thus the episodic baseline retained decision evidence;
it was not reduced to source reports alone.

Model, system prompt, neutral style, output contract and settings were common.
Conditions rotated order deterministically. Maximum memory was 2,000ordinary-cl100k
tokens, with system/question/output framing separate. Temperature 1 / top_p 0.95,
reasoning effort low, maximum 2,048 provider output tokens including thinking;
JSON choice plus concise justification. No hidden reasoning transcript was
requested. One sample per condition/checkpoint, no retries, substitutions or
paid repairs. Exact choice grading and error-as-wrong rules were frozen before
execution. Expected answers and grading references never entered requests.
